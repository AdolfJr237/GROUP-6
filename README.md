# Group 6 Inventory Restocking System

An explainable, AI-assisted inventory management and restocking prototype for small businesses.

## Project scope

The prototype will:

- Manage products, batches, sales, suppliers, and stock movements.
- Forecast short-term demand with a baseline and one machine-learning model.
- Estimate stock-out and expiry risk.
- Recommend manager-approved reorder dates and quantities under supplier lead-time and budget constraints.
- Provide a read-only AI assistant grounded in validated inventory data.
